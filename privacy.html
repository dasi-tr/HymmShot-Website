Privacy information for the HymmShot website, including analytics and third-party links.

Website data and analytics

The website does not include account registration, contact forms or advertising scripts. The homepage uses Google Analytics / Google tag to measure site usage; Google may process device, browser and interaction information under its own privacy terms. No additional tracking has been added for HymmShot 1.0.0.

Third-party services

Downloads, source-code links and feedback links lead to GitHub. The footer also links to LinkedIn. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Desktop application

This notice describes the website package published at hymmshot.com. Information about any future diagnostics, telemetry or network features in the Windows application should be documented with the relevant release.
