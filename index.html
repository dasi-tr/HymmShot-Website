Questions and answers

What is HymmShot?

HymmShot is a family of focused screenshot tools for Windows. Each tool does one screenshot job well. Right now the family includes HymmScroll, for scrolling screenshots, and HymmStack, for combining several captures into one image.

Which tool do I need, HymmScroll or HymmStack?

If the content is in one place but longer than your screen, like a long web page, chat or log, use HymmScroll. If the content is spread across different places, like several steps of a process or a few different windows, use HymmStack.

Do HymmShot tools upload my screenshots anywhere?

No. Capturing and editing happen on your own computer, and the tools don't need an account or a cloud service to work. That makes them a good fit for work environments with strict security rules.

Are the tools free?

HymmScroll is free during its Public Beta. HymmStack's availability and pricing will be listed on Microsoft Store when it launches. Any future pricing change will be announced clearly in advance.

Where do I download them?

From Microsoft Store. It's the official, signed version, and Windows keeps it up to date for you.

Is HymmScroll the same app that used to be called HymmShot?

Yes. The scrolling screenshot app was renamed to HymmScroll when HymmShot became the name of the whole family. Same app, same features.

Can I use HymmShot tools at work?

Yes. They run locally on Windows, and the built-in blur tool helps you hide customer data or internal details before sharing. For questions about using them in your organization, email contact@hymmshot.com.

I found a bug or have an idea. How do I reach you?

Email contact@hymmshot.com. Feedback from real use shapes what gets built next.
