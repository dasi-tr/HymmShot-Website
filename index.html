Questions and answers

What is HymmShot?

HymmShot is a family of focused screenshot tools for Windows. Each tool is built around one specific capture workflow, so you can use the right tool for the job instead of one overloaded app.

Which tool do I need?

If the content is in one place but longer than your screen, like a long web page, chat or log, use HymmScroll. If the content is spread across different places, like several steps of a process or a few different windows, use HymmStack.

Do HymmShot tools upload my screenshots?

No. Capturing and editing happen on your own computer, and the tools don't need an account or a cloud service to work. That makes them a good fit for work environments with strict security rules.

Where do I download them?

From Microsoft Store. HymmScroll is available now and free during its Public Beta. HymmStack is coming soon, and its Store listing will be linked here.
